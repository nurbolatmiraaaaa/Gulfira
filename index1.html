Modernism in Literature

Exploring To the Lighthouse by Virginia Woolf

What is Modernism?

Modernism was not just a literary movement, but a complete departure from the ordered, logical world of the 19th century. It emphasized dislocation, fragmented identity, and inner experience.

"She felt this thing that she called life terrible, hostile, and quick to pounce..." – V. Woolf

Key Modernist Themes

  • Fragmentation: The narrative splits and shifts unpredictably.
  • Stream of Consciousness: Unfiltered thoughts dominate narration.
  • Anti-traditionalism: Plot and form take second place to feeling.
